Welcome to Your Journey

This companion guide brings the timeless wisdom of Shantideva's Way of the Bodhisattva into your daily life. The Tibetan word gom means "to become familiar"—this is a practice of returning again and again, until wisdom moves from concept into lived experience.

"When loving-kindness and compassion become unlimited, vast, and really deep, that is what we call bodhicitta."

Guided Practices (PRAC Series)

These six practices form the foundation of your daily cultivation. Each one brings a teaching from concept into lived experience.

PRAC-L1: Bodhicitta Motivation

Begin each day by setting the intention to benefit all beings.

Practice:

Sit quietly and reflect on the suffering of others, letting natural compassion arise. Then formally take the aspiration to use this day, this life, for the welfare of all.

Steps:

1. Take three deep breaths to settle
2. Bring to mind the suffering in the world
3. Notice the natural wish for beings to be free from pain
4. Speak the aspiration: "May this day benefit all beings"
5. Let this intention infuse your actions throughout the day

PRAC-L2: Welcoming Elements

Practice complete hospitality toward your experience.

Practice:

Sit in meditation and welcome whatever arises. When a thought comes, welcome it. When an emotion comes, welcome it. When a sensation comes, welcome it.

Steps:

1. Settle into a comfortable posture
2. As each experience arises, silently say "welcome"
3. Notice the shift from resistance to receptivity
4. Let everything be a guest in the spacious inn of awareness
5. Practice for 15-20 minutes

PRAC-L3: Patience & Joyful Effort

Choose one small practice and do it with joy.

Practice:

Not forcing, not striving, but with genuine interest and delight. Notice when effort becomes strain, and return to natural joy.

Steps:

1. Choose a simple practice (breath awareness, walking, journaling)
2. Begin with curiosity rather than obligation
3. Notice when you're pushing—pause and soften
4. Find the natural delight in simply being present
5. Let joy be the fuel, not willpower

PRAC-L4: Recognizing Basic Goodness

This awareness itself is basic goodness—already here.

Practice:

Sit and rest in awareness. Notice the simple fact of being conscious, aware, present. Not something to achieve, but to recognize.

Steps:

1. Settle and let breath find its rhythm
2. Notice: you are aware right now
3. This awareness is not broken or lacking
4. Rest in the simple fact of knowing
5. This is basic goodness—complete as it is

PRAC-L5: Investigating Self (Analytical)

Where is this "I"? Look carefully.

Practice:

Ask: Where is this "I"? Is it in the body? In the mind? In thoughts? In feelings? When you look directly, can you find a solid, permanent self anywhere?

Steps:

1. Sit quietly and notice the sense of "I"
2. Look in the body—is "I" here?
3. Look in thoughts—is "I" these passing words?
4. Look in feelings—is "I" these shifting sensations?
5. Rest in the gap of not-finding

PRAC-L6: Investigating Self (Temporal)

If the self changes constantly, where is the permanent "I"?

Practice:

Notice that everything changes. The you of yesterday is not the you of today. The you of this morning is not the you of this moment.

Steps:

1. Recall yourself from a year ago—how different?
2. Notice yourself from this morning—already changed?
3. Feel the constant flux of sensation, thought, mood
4. Where in this river of change is the permanent "I"?
5. Rest in the seeing of constant transformation
